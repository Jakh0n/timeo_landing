Timeo is restaurant staff scheduling.

1. A manager signs up, names the restaurant, and adds branches.
2. They create a shift requirement: branch, weekday and weekend, day and night, and how many people are needed.
3. The app creates one public link. Workers have no accounts.
4. A worker opens the link, enters their name and employee ID, which days they can work, day or night, and a time range.
5. Submitting again for the same employee ID replaces the previous submission.
6. The manager generates a schedule, reviews it, and confirms it.
7. The worker opens the same link, enters the same name and employee ID, and sees their assigned shifts.
